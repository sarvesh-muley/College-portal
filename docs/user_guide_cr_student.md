# CR & Student Guide: College Portal

Welcome to the College Portal. This guide explains how to use the interactive dashboard for both general students and Class Representatives (CRs).

## 🎓 For Every Student

### 1. Interactive Timetable
- View your **upcoming 3-day schedule** at a glance.
- The timetable is personalized based on your **Branch** and **Enrollments**.
- **Unified Events**: Regular classes (Indigo), special events (Amber), and academic notices (Teal) all appear in one integrated grid.

### 2. Events & Notices
- **Live Slideshow**: Catch important announcements and high-resolution posters at the top of your dashboard.
- **Click-to-Zoom**: Click any event poster to open a high-resolution, full-screen viewer.
- **Academic Timeline**: Scrolldown to see a history of academic notices sorted by date.
- **Read Full Notice**: Use the "Info" button on any notice to see the unabridged message, specific dates, and whom it was posted by.

### 3. Personal Profile
- Access via **My Profile** in the sidebar.
- **Contact Details**: Keep your mobile number and residential address updated.
- **Custom Avatar**: Personalize your portal by pasting an image URL into the Avatar field.
- *Note: Your Name and Academic records (Branch/Semester) are managed by the office and cannot be edited manually.*

---

## 📢 For Class Representatives (CRs)
CRs have access to the **Content Tools** (Publisher) section.

### 1. Publishing Notices
- **Scope Selection**: 
  - **Specific Section**: Target only your enrolled course (e.g., CS101 Section A).
  - **Entire Batch**: Target everyone in your Branch and Semester.
- **Category Matters**: 
  - **Academic Notice**: These appear in the timeline (text-focused).
  - **Event Notice**: These appear in the big slideshow (requires an Image URL).
- **Attribution**: Your name is automatically cited on every notice you post.

### 2. Managing Content
- Scroll to the bottom of your tools to find **Manage Your Notices**.
- **Edit**: Fixing a typo or extending an end date? Use the pencil icon to open the edit modal.
- **Delete**: Remove outdated or mistake posts instantly using the trash icon.

### 3. Assignment Tracking
- Use the **Open Assignment** form to notify students of new deadlines.
- Define the course, section, and due date. These appear specifically in the "Assignments" sidebar for relevant students.

---

## 📱 Mobile Experience
- The portal is fully responsive.
- **Timetable Smart Scroll**: On mobile devices, the broad timetable grid can be scrolled horizontally to ensure readability on small screens.
